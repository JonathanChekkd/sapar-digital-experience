Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: high-fidelity north-star mobile app screen, 390 x 844 portrait

The user lands on PULSE and immediately sees community, an upcoming training session, a verified match story, and clear navigation. This is a shippable interface mockup, not concept art and not a poster.

Visual world: bright daylight, optimistic full palette—electric cobalt as dominant field, fresh green, tomato coral, amber, warm cream, and charcoal ink. Original polished stylized 3D/cartoon adult athletes with realistic adult proportions and respectful training attire. Tactile illuminated controls inspired by premium arcade hardware, with layered physical depth and satisfying press surfaces but no gambling cues. Selective translucent glass only over the hero artwork. Full-bleed color fields and large cream slab, not a grid of same-size cards.
Type and hierarchy: highly legible geometric grotesque UI type, bold flush-left heading, fixed mobile UI scale, tabular numerals. Accessibility: WCAG-like contrast, 44px controls, no critical meaning by color alone, calm success state, clear focus/pressed affordances.
Constraints: original design only; no Instagram, Clash, chess.com, Jits.gg, UFC, casino, slot machine, or other brand trade dress; no copied characters, card frames, maps, layouts, icons, wording, logos, ratings, or athlete likenesses; no blood, injury, minors, belts inferred from ratings, fake scarcity, loot boxes, random rewards, streak loss, or medical claims; no watermark; no device mockup; render one straight-on full mobile screen with practical UI and readable hierarchy.
